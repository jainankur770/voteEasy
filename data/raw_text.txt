Voting is a fundamental right in India. To vote, you must be an Indian citizen and be 18 years of age or older. You need to register on the electoral roll, which can be done via the Voter Helpline App or voters.eci.gov.in using Form 6. When voting, carry your EPIC (Voter ID) or an alternative approved ID like Aadhaar or PAN card. Use the EVM and verify your vote on the VVPAT machine. For more details, visit the Election Commission of India website.
